Jordan’s Adventure Club

Privacy and family choices

Effective November 14, 2026 · Notice jac-2026-11-14-v1

Jordan’s Adventure Club is a private, invitation-based family adventure and reading app operated by Walter Clay. It is a personal project, unrelated to the U.S. Department of the Interior. Contact wclay24@gmail.com with privacy questions, account-access problems, guardian disputes or deletion requests. Do not email passwords, identity documents, children’s photos or unnecessary sensitive information.

Open Privacy Center to export your information, manage child participation, pause adult AI processing, remove records or submit a request. Sign-in is required for private records.

For parents and guardians

An adult creates the child’s profile and issues a short-lived, single-use pairing code. The child does not register, supply an email address or choose a password. A profile can support adult planning without pairing a child’s device. Pairing gives one device restricted access to one child’s experience. You can disconnect devices, withdraw child participation, review records and request deletion.

Use a nickname and age rather than a full name or birthday. Optional settings include interests, reading support and school district. A school name, student ID and attendance records are not required. District calendars show public school dates, not a family’s actual availability. Parents choose browsing and sharing settings; turning twelve does not automatically expand access.

When paired, children can express adventure wishes and save optional reflections. The original household also supports photo submission for completed shared adventures. Parents should supervise uploads and avoid sensitive content. Wishes are not bookings. Family managers can view and edit the space’s private records; viewers can read and export permitted family records. Neither a family invitation nor a pairing code independently verifies legal guardianship.

New child profiles require an adult acknowledgment of guardian authority and this notice. Existing private family access is preserved until changed; a versioned acknowledgment can be recorded in Privacy Center. This acknowledgment is not independent identity verification or a completed public-app parental-consent process. Withdrawal disconnects child devices and blocks new pairing until the family owner acknowledges again. Export and deletion remain available.

Information and purposes

  • Adult accounts: invitation email, optional display name, password hash, permissions, authentication records and optional passkey public keys or protected authenticator configuration, used for sign-in and security.
  • Child and family records: parent-entered profiles, preferences, wishes, outing decisions, optional written memories, reading observations, reflections and photos, used for private planning and the child experience.
  • Adult planning: private conversations, saved drafts, preferences, optional home starting point and day plans, used for the features you choose.
  • Operations: temporary session cookies, short-lived abuse-prevention records, request and deletion receipts, limited delivery records and service logs, used for access, reliability, security and privacy requests. Hosting providers may process network addresses and request metadata.

The app does not sell personal information, serve targeted advertisements, use cross-app advertising trackers or perform face recognition or voice identification. It does not ask children for precise location, contacts, microphone access or a public profile. Optional reading observations are family notes, not diagnoses or assessments of a child.

Family access and sharing

Independent families have separate spaces. App ownership alone does not grant access to another family’s records; the operator’s limited privacy-request inbox supports responding to requests. Explicit viewer or manager grants share that family’s records, including export access. Review grants in Families and adult permissions in Admin. The original household keeps its existing owner/co-parent access. Child photos and stories are shown only through the applicable sharing and visibility checks. Exported copies and content someone already received cannot be recalled by removing an app permission.

Photos

Photos are privately stored in Google Cloud Storage and served through authenticated app routes. The app decodes and re-encodes uploads to remove embedded EXIF/GPS metadata; visible faces, text or landmarks can still identify people. Photos default to unshared except original child-submitted photos within an already-visible completed adventure. Share only what the intended children and authorized family adults should see.

Removing a photo immediately removes its serving record and queues deletion of its live storage object. The existing worker retries failures through the app’s private storage connection; you can also retry a due job from Privacy Center. The app checks live-object absence before marking that job complete. Provider versions, retention holds, soft-deleted objects, backups and previously downloaded copies have separate lifecycles and are not certified erased by that check. Interrupted uploads are reconciled through an upload-intent record; older storage orphans require an operator inventory review.

Adult AI

AIE chat is for signed-in adults. When you send a message, the app sends bounded conversation history, public event information, adult preferences and permitted selected planning fields to OpenAI. Automatically sourced child identities, wishes, reading settings and observations, private journals and photos are excluded. Public district calendars are selected from districts you explicitly name; they are not inferred from a child’s profile. Traffic Logic supplies only redacted timing, stop types and warnings, not addresses or GPS.

Known stored child names and common identifiers in adult free text are redacted as an additional safeguard. Detection is imperfect: do not enter children’s identities, health details, home addresses or other sensitive personal information. The app does not provide the model arbitrary account, photo, journal or messaging access. Free-text notes from saved planning cases are not automatically sent.

AI requests use store=false. This does not establish approved Zero Data Retention or erase provider security logs. See OpenAI API data controls. Pause your account’s AI processing in Privacy Center; queued requests are stopped and subsequent streaming delivery is checked against withdrawal. Previously transmitted data cannot be recalled by pausing. Saved app records remain until deleted or expired.

Location, routes and calendars

Current location is requested only when an adult selects the location control and grants browser permission. There is no continuous tracking; current-position coordinates are removed before a day plan is saved. Manual entry remains available. Saved home and itinerary inputs are encrypted in the app database. Clear a saved home field or delete a private day plan in Plans.

If Google Maps routing is configured and you explicitly request predictions, the app sends the necessary start and stop locations, departure times and routing preferences to Google under its Privacy Policy. The app does not save a historical database of Google route predictions. Opening a page does not request a route. Live traffic requires separate provider setup and is not established by this notice.

Public school calendars are fetched from official district sites without child identities. Explicit calendar delivery may send outing details through the configured email service to your chosen Skylight calendar; imported copies follow that provider’s controls. No booking or calendar delivery is performed just by chatting or saving an ordinary draft.

Service providers and outside links

Render hosts the application, database and background job; Google Cloud Storage holds private photos; OpenAI processes configured adult AI and public event research; Google Maps is used only for an enabled, requested route check. Configured email providers process invitations, recovery and explicitly enabled notifications. Provider access, retention and deletion terms require operator review. We do not claim that app deletion erases provider backups, email already delivered, or AI data already transmitted. Organizer, ticket and restaurant links open outside services under their own policies.

Retention

  • Original household choice and submission history: 90 days under the existing cleanup job.
  • Idle AI conversations: removed after 90 days without a new turn. Unresolved queued, running or uncertain operations are retained for reconciliation; deliberately saved planning drafts remain until deleted.
  • Family profiles, plans, memories, photos, wishes and optional observations: retained for the ongoing family feature until removed. Remove individual records in Families or use family deletion.
  • Family/access audit entries and AI notices: 90 days. Expired pairing, invitation, recovery and passkey challenges are cleaned by the worker; expired email-outbox records are removed after 30 days.
  • Resolved or denied privacy-request content: 365 days after the latest response. Open requests remain for follow-up. Minimal deletion receipts, object-job records and spending reservations are restricted operational records retained for restoration safeguards and reconciliation, without deleted transcripts or photos.
  • Provider backups and service logs: subject to separately verified provider policies. Restored databases must remain isolated until deletion and withdrawal records have been replayed.

Access, correction, export and deletion

Privacy Center supports account archives and family archives with actual authorized photo bytes. Password reauthentication and enabled authenticator protection apply. A family archive that cannot retrieve a photo fails rather than claiming a complete export. The 50 MB photo limit requires individual downloads or an operator-assisted export for larger collections. Protect downloaded archives.

Edit profiles and family records in Families. Family owners can delete a child or entire family; adults can clear their personal data or delete an invited adult account. Child deletion also removes all photos and written journals in that family because the app cannot reliably identify who appears in untagged media; the confirmation explains this broader effect. Linked outing records are removed. Export first if you want a copy. Operator-account closure requires a successor or service shutdown; request it through Privacy Center or email. Personal data and the original family can still be removed without resetting the operator login.

Submit access, correction, deletion, appeal or other privacy questions in Privacy Center, or email the operator if you cannot sign in. Requests are reviewed in the app; submitting one does not send an email. Identity and guardian authority may need proportionate verification. An operator response is not proof that a provider action occurred. Applicable rights, exceptions and deadlines depend on the request and jurisdiction; we do not limit requests to California residents.

For children

Your grown-up chooses who can use this club. You can read, pick adventures and share a memory with your family. Ask your grown-up before adding a photo. Do not put your address, school, passwords or secrets in a memory. If you want something removed or feel uncomfortable, tell your grown-up.

Security, changes and future public availability

The app uses HTTPS in production, restricted family access, protected sessions and server-held credentials. Adults can enable passkeys and authenticator protection. No system can promise perfect security. Report suspected unauthorized access using the contact above.

This release remains a private web application. Public registration, commercial distribution and an iPhone release require additional legal applicability review, suitable verified parental-consent implementation where required, vendor and backup assurance, and platform-specific privacy work. This notice is not a compliance certification. Material changes will update the version and may require renewed choices before a new use.
