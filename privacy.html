General Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This notice describes general privacy practices for applications, prototypes, integrations, and other projects maintained by wclay24. Project-specific notices may provide additional details and control if they are more specific.

Information that may be processed

Depending on the project, information may include user-provided content, account identifiers, authentication data, application configuration, and data returned by connected services. The specific information processed depends on the feature the user chooses to use.

Purpose of processing

Information is processed only as needed to provide requested functionality, authenticate authorized connections, retrieve permitted data, perform user-requested analysis, generate user-requested outputs, maintain security, and diagnose operational errors.

Connected services

Some projects may connect to third-party services through official APIs or authorized integration mechanisms. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the user or service provider. A connected service's own terms and privacy practices also apply.

Data minimization

Projects are designed to use the minimum information reasonably necessary for the requested task. Unnecessary sensitive information should not be submitted.

Credentials and secrets

API keys, access tokens, client secrets, and similar credentials should be stored in secure server-side secret management or environment configuration and should not be committed to public source repositories.

Data sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be transmitted to connected services when necessary to perform a user-requested function and only within the authorization available to the application.

Retention

Retention depends on the project architecture and connected service requirements. Temporary operational data may be retained only as needed to provide functionality, troubleshoot issues, comply with applicable requirements, or maintain security.

User choices

Users may choose not to connect optional external services. Users may also revoke permissions through the relevant external service where supported.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce unauthorized access, including server-side secret storage, least-privilege access, controlled integrations, and separation of public source code from credentials.

Changes

This notice may be updated when project capabilities, data practices, or integrations materially change. The effective date will be revised when updates are published.

Contact

For privacy questions related to these projects, contact: wclay24@gmail.com.
