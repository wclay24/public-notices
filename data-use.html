Data Use Notice

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Applications maintained by wclay24 are intended to process only the information needed to provide user-requested functionality.

Core principles

  • Use the minimum information necessary for the requested function.
  • Do not treat missing information as permission to invent facts.
  • Do not expose secrets, tokens, or API keys in public repositories or outputs.
  • Use official APIs and authorized integration mechanisms where available.
  • Do not substitute scraping for permissions that have not been granted.
  • Do not sell personal information.
  • Keep external-service data use within the permissions granted by the user and service provider.

Public data

Some applications may retrieve public information, such as public job announcements, to support user-requested analysis. Public availability does not eliminate the need for appropriate source attribution, currentness checks, and responsible use.

User-supplied data

User-supplied documents and statements may be analyzed for the requested purpose. Users remain responsible for deciding what information they choose to submit.
